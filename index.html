Exclusive EVENTKN

Exclusive EVENTKN is a luxury event planning company dedicated to designing and curating experiences. We specialize in bringing thoughtful creativity, refined aesthetics, and seamless planning to private and milestone events. We are based in Kano and available for travel.

Our core services include weddings and bridal events (including traditional and bridal ceremony styling), birthday parties and kids' celebrations (themed parties, decor, and styling), favours and curated guest experiences, and full event design and decor curation.
